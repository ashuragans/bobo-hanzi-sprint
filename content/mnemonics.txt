備|亻 person + 𤰇|A person (亻) with everything ready: prepared. 準備.
典|冊 book + 丌 stand|A book (冊) on a stand (丌): classic book. 字典 = dictionary.
總|糸 thread + 悤|All the threads (糸) gathered: total, always. 總是 = always.
業|a musical instrument stand|A rack: business, studies. 作業 = homework.
李|木 tree + 子 child|A tree (木) with fruit like children (子): plum; the surname Li. 李先生.
員|口 + 貝 money|A person who handles money (貝): member, staff. 店員 = shop clerk.
造|辶 walk + 告|Walking (辶) to tell (告) people what you built: to make. 造句 = make a sentence.
臥|臣 + 人 person|A person (人) lying down: to lie down. 臥室 = bedroom.
約|糸 thread + 勺 ladle|Tying (糸) things together: agreement, about. 大約 = about.
正|一 + 止 stop|Stopping (止) right at the line (一): correct, just. 正在 = right now (doing).
內|冂 + 入 enter|Entering (入) the space (冂): inside. 內衣 = underwear.
桃|木 tree + 兆|A fruit tree (木): peach. 桃園 = Taoyuan.
通|辶 walk + 甬 passage|Walk (辶) down a long passage (甬) and come out the other side: go through, connect. 交通 = traffic, transportation.
華|艹 flowers + 𠦒 branches|A tree whose every branch (𠦒) bursts into flowers (艹): splendid — the old proud name for China. 華語 = Chinese.
碰|石 stone + 並 side by side|Two stones (石) set side by side (並) knock together — clack!: bump into, touch. 不要碰 = don't touch.
型|刑 cut a mold + 土 earth|Clay (土) pressed into a mold cut with a knife (刑): a model, a pattern. 句型 = sentence pattern.
主|丶 flame + 王 lamp stand|The flame (丶) sits on top of the lamp stand (王); whoever lights the lamp is the master of the house: main, master. 主詞 = subject.
詞|言 words + 司 manager|The manager (司) picks out the words (言) one by one: a word. 名詞 = noun, 動詞 = verb.
形|开 frame + 彡 brush streaks|Brush streaks (彡) drawn on a frame (开) give it a shape: shape, form. 形容詞 = adjective (a word that gives things a shape).
抽|扌 hand + 由 from|A hand (扌) pulls something out from (由) a box: draw out. 抽菸 = to smoke (draw on a cigarette).
菸|艹 plant + 於 at|A plant (艹) whose leaves end up rolled and lit: tobacco. 抽菸 = to smoke. 不可抽菸 = no smoking.
之|a foot stepping on a winding path|A little path that winds from one place to another: "of" in old Chinese, and 之間 = between (the path between two places).
完|宀 roof + 元 whole|Under the roof (宀) everything is whole (元) again — the job is done: finished, complete. 完成 = to complete.
答|⺮ bamboo + 合 fit together|Two bamboo (⺮) slips that fit together (合), like a question and its reply: answer. 回答 = to answer.
